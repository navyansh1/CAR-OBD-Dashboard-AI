Cracking Hyundai's hidden engine data

Beyond the standard readings every car shares, your Creta's engine computer has 13 secret data blocks that Hyundai doesn't document. To find out what's inside, the dashboard watches every number in them while the engine revs and the car drives. When one moves in perfect step with something we already know, like engine speed, we've cracked it.

Turn this on, then rev the engine in neutral or go for a short drive.
